# Introduction {#sec:introduction}

**[SKELETON: this section states the proposition and the plan.]**

The proposition: *time is a flat circle*. Everything that happens has happened before and will
happen again, in the same order, without end.

The line is from the first season of *True Detective* [@noble2014truedetective]. The idea is
older. Nietzsche used it as a test of a life [@nietzsche1910joyful].

To argue:

- The proposition has two readings. One is a claim about the world. The other is a question
  about how to live.
- The essay takes each reading in turn and asks what would make it true.
- Plan: [@Sec:demon] gives the ethical reading, [@Sec:physics] the physical one,
  [@Sec:formal] a formal statement, [@Sec:objections] the objections, and [@Sec:conclusion]
  the answer.
