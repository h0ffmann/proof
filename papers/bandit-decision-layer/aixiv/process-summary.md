The decision layer was first specified in Lean 4, then checked with Z3 for up to five arms, and is now specified in Agda 2.8.0 with its standard library 2.3, under `--safe --without-K`, for every number of arms. Claude Code ported the definitions and proofs to Agda under the author's direction and wrote a first draft of this paper from the Agda files; every written proof follows the corresponding Agda proof (Table 1 of the paper maps each result to its Agda name). The Haskell implementation mirrors the Agda names and its first output recomputes the Agda examples.
